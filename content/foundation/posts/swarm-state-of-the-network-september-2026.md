### **September 2026 State of the Network Report**

This report provides an overview of the Swarm network's key metrics for September 2026, including rewards, participation levels, and node distribution, and places them in context with recent monthly trends.

{{< admonition info >}}
**_Active nodes_** are full nodes identified by a unique Gnosis Chain address with the required minimum staked xBZZ that have submitted a [commit](https://docs.ethswarm.org/docs/concepts/incentives/redistribution-game/#redistribution-game-details) transaction during the month.
{{< /admonition >}}

{{< admonition info >}}
All rewards are denominated in xBZZ. Since the fiat value of xBZZ fluctuates monthly, these metrics may not reflect the real-world returns for node operators.
{{< /admonition >}}

## Conclusion

In September, reward-related metrics continued to rise: total network rewards, median and average win values, and average earnings per active staking node all increased relative to August, with total rewards recording a fifth consecutive monthly increase and both total rewards and average earnings per node reaching their highest levels in the period shown. Participation also increased across all three counts — active staking nodes, total reachable full nodes, and nodes winning at least once each rose from August, with active staking nodes recording their first monthly increase in the period shown. Geographically, active staking remained concentrated in Finland and the broader population of reachable full nodes continued to be led by Germany, consistent with recent months.
