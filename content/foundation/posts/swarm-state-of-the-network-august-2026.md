### **August 2026 State of the Network Report**

This report provides an overview of the Swarm network's key metrics for August 2026, including rewards, participation levels, and node distribution, and places them in context with recent monthly trends.

{{< admonition info >}}
**_Active nodes_** are full nodes identified by a unique Gnosis Chain address with the required minimum staked xBZZ that have submitted a [commit](https://docs.ethswarm.org/docs/concepts/incentives/redistribution-game/#redistribution-game-details) transaction during the month.
{{< /admonition >}}

{{< admonition info >}}
All rewards are denominated in xBZZ. Since the fiat value of xBZZ fluctuates monthly, these metrics may not reflect the real-world returns for node operators.
{{< /admonition >}}

## Conclusion

In August, reward-related metrics continued to rise: total network rewards, median and average win values, and average earnings per active staking node all increased relative to July, with total rewards recording a fourth consecutive monthly increase and average earnings per node reaching their highest level in the period shown. Participation edged lower across all three counts — active staking nodes, total reachable full nodes, and nodes winning at least once each declined modestly from July. Geographically, active staking remained concentrated in Finland and the broader population of reachable full nodes continued to be led by Germany, consistent with recent months.
